How to Download Videos on Android, iPhone, and PC

Android

  1. Copy the video link from the Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok app.
  2. Open SimpanClip in Chrome, paste the link, and press Download.
  3. Pick a quality. The file is saved in your Downloads folder and shows up in Gallery or Files.

iPhone (iOS)

  1. Use Safari. Other browsers may fail to save the file.
  2. Paste the link in SimpanClip, choose a quality, and tap Download.
  3. Tap the download icon in Safari, open the file in the Files app, then tap Share and Save Video to add it to Photos.

Computer (Windows, Mac, Linux)

  1. Copy the video address from your browser's address bar.
  2. Paste it into SimpanClip, choose a quality, and click Download.
  3. The file is saved to your browser's Downloads folder.

Tip: HD videos take longer because picture and sound are merged on the server first.
